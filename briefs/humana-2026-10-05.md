## 12. ROI Sketch
`annual_calls × automatable_share × containment × (human − AI cost per call)`
- Annual calls: **80M** (sourced [2])
- Automatable share: 30–50% (assumed)
- Containment: 50–70% (assumed)
- Human cost/call: $4–$8 (assumed); AI cost/call: $0.50–$1.50 (assumed)

**Conservative:** 80M × 30% × 50% × $3 ≈ **$36M/yr**. **Midpoint:** 80M × 40% × 60% × $5 ≈ **$96M/yr**.
Over three years, even the conservative case (~$108M) matches Humana's entire Agent Assist savings target ($100M+ over a few years) [1]. That gives you a frame their leadership has already approved.
