Create a **one-page marketing slick** (US Letter, portrait, 8.5 × 11 in, print-ready and PDF-friendly) from **Deepgram** for **Southern Company**. It is a leave-behind and email attachment for customer-experience and technology leaders at Southern Company Gas, Georgia Power, and Southern Company. It should read in 60 seconds and make one point: **Deepgram brings Southern Company's AI investments to the phone, starting with the calls that spike on the worst-weather days.**

## Design direction
- Use the Deepgram brand: a dark, near-black background (or a dark header band on a light page if that prints better) with a bright Deepgram-green accent and clean sans-serif type. If a Deepgram design system is available, use it instead of these notes.
- Use a clear visual hierarchy: headline → 3 value columns → use-case strip → proof → how to start → call to action. Leave generous white space. **About 250 words total.** No paragraph longer than 2 lines.
- Header: Deepgram logo placeholder on the left. On the right, small text: "Prepared for Southern Company" with a placeholder box labeled "Southern Company logo". **Don't recreate either company's logo.** Don't imply a partnership, endorsement, or existing customer relationship.
- Visual motif: a subtle sound-wave line that turns into a power-line or grid line across the header.
- Use only the facts given here. **Do not invent metrics, customer names, logos, or quotes.** Mark anything unverified as `[verify]`.
- Tone: confident, warm, and respectful of Southern Company's existing investments. Never criticize their vendors, IVR, wait times, or service.

## Content, top to bottom

### 1. Headline band
- Headline: **"When the storm hits, every caller gets an answer."**
- Subhead: "Deepgram voice AI brings your customer data and AI investments to the phone, so routine calls get resolved instantly and your representatives focus on the customers who need them most."

### 2. Three value columns (icon + title + one line each)
- **Understands every caller.** Accurate on account numbers, addresses, and regional accents, the details that decide whether a call resolves.
- **Feels like a conversation.** Real-time, low-latency speech with natural turn-taking, not "press 1."
- **Fits what you already run.** Works with Five9, Amazon Connect, Genesys, and Twilio, and with your own LLMs and customer data. Deploys in the cloud, your VPC, or on-premises.

### 3. "Built for utility calls" strip
A row of 5 small icon tiles, labeled:
- Outage status & restoration updates
- Bill & balance questions
- Payment arrangements
- Start, stop & move service
- Energy-assistance information

Caption under the strip: "Your agents get every call that needs a person, with full context passed along."

### 4. Proof (two tiles side by side)
- **2× authentication rates.** A major healthcare provider doubled caller authentication after switching its Five9 virtual agent to Deepgram, which was 2–4× more accurate on alphanumeric inputs. *(Source: deepgram.com/customers/five9)*
- **100,000+ calls a month.** Abby Connect runs an AI receptionist on Deepgram, with 5× QA productivity. *(Source: deepgram.com/learn/case-study/abby-connect)*

### 5. Deepgram at a glance (thin stat bar)
**200,000+ developers** · **1,400+ organizations, 400+ enterprises** · **50,000+ years of audio processed** · **SOC 2 Type II · HIPAA (BAA) · PCI DSS · GDPR**

### 6. How to start (3-step horizontal flow)
1. **Proof (2–3 weeks):** run your own recorded calls through Deepgram and compare accuracy, latency, and cost, inside your environment.
2. **Pilot (8–10 weeks):** a voice agent on 2–3 high-volume routine calls for one utility, with warm handoff to your representatives.
3. **Storm-ready:** scale to more call types and utilities before the next hurricane season.
