## TL;DR (read this in the elevator)
- **Who they are:** London-based enterprise voice-agent vendor ("The Agentic Dialog Platform"). 100+ enterprise customers (Marriott, Caesars, PG&E, UniCredit, FedEx) across 25 countries and 75 languages. $200M+ raised; Series D Dec 2025 at a reported ~$750M valuation [8][13][23].
- **Why now:** PolyAI is moving its speech layer **in-house**. Owl ASR is built on NVIDIA Riva [4][5], and Dialog-RSN-1 (Jul 30, 2026) merges speech recognition into an audio-native model, but it is **English only** [6][7]. Every other language still runs through third-party ASR, and **Deepgram is already one of the providers wired into the platform** [2][3].
- **#1 pain hypothesis:** *🧠 Hypothesis:* the multilingual long tail (non-English, fallback, and self-serve builders) runs on a patchwork of seven ASR vendors and seven TTS vendors. That raises cost, makes quality uneven, and makes latency hard to control [2][15].
- **Motion:** **OEM / embed (expand).** This is a vendor, not an end customer. Grow Deepgram's share of PolyAI's third-party ASR routing (non-English and fallback), and get **Aura** added to a TTS list that today has no Deepgram voice [15]. Not a displacement of Owl or Dialog-RSN-1.
- **Person to win:** **Paul Asoyan, VP Strategic Alliances & BD** (joined May 2026, ex-Google, ex-Gong partnerships) [11] as the door. **Helen Greul, SVP Engineering** [11] and **Shawn Wen, CTO** [5][22] decide.
- **Ask this one question:** "For calls Owl and RSN-1 don't cover, how do you choose the ASR provider for each language, and what share of minutes goes to each today?"
