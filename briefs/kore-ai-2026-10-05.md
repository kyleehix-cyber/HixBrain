## TL;DR
- **Who:** Kore.ai, Inc. is a private conversational and agentic AI platform. It is a Gartner MQ Leader for the fourth time [11] and claims 500+ large enterprises and 20B+ interactions per year (vendor claim) [19]. It is already a **Deepgram partner**: Nova-3 and Aura-2 are native speech providers in its platform [1].
- **Why now:** The Artemis edition of the Kore.ai Agent Platform (May 2026) is built natively on Azure, including **Azure Speech Services** [6][8]. A new Chief Commercial Officer (July 2026) [10] and fresh AllianceBernstein capital (Jan 2026) [3] add urgency.
- **Pain:** *🧠* Deepgram is one of several selectable speech engines [13]. As Artemis standardizes on Azure, Azure Speech becomes the path of least resistance, and Deepgram's share of Kore.ai voice traffic is at risk.
- **Motion:** **OEM / embed plus partner co-sell.** Make Deepgram the *default* STT/TTS (and real-time voice layer) in Artemis, then co-sell into Kore.ai's banking and healthcare voice deals.
- **Win:** Prasanna Arikala (CTO and Head of Products) on the architecture, and Uma Sandilya (CCO) on joint go-to-market [10][18].
- **Ask:** "In Artemis, which speech engine is the default for a new voice agent, and what would it take for that to be Deepgram?"

## 6. Talk Track & Objections
- **Opener:** "Artemis is Azure-first, and that's fine: Deepgram runs inside your Azure tenancy. Let's make sure the speech layer under your voice agents is the one that wins your banking and payer bake-offs."
- **Angles:** *CTO:* default engine, latency, barge-in, VPC deployment, Voice Agent API for real-time agents. *CCO:* joint pipeline in healthcare and banking; Deepgram brings the speech proof points. *Customer Ops:* fewer accuracy escalations on IDs, names, and accents.
- **Proof:** Five9 healthcare (2x authentication); Elerian AI (on-prem banking, >90% precision).

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We're Azure-native now" | Deepgram can run in your VPC, so audio never leaves Azure. Compare accuracy, latency, and cost per hour on your customers' audio |
| "Customers pick their own engine" | Defaults drive most choices. Make the best-tested engine the default and keep the choice |
| "Speech is a commodity" | Alphanumerics and accents decide containment. Five9 saw 2x authentication |
| "Margin / pricing" | Lower cost at scale than hyperscaler speech; structure an OEM rate on volume |
